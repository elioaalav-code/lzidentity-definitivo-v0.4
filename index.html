[ 03 ] MANIFEST · ONE YOU, EVERYWHERE

One you.
Everywhere.

Today, every chain treats you like a stranger. You connect, you prove yourself, you start over. New wallet on Base, new name on Solana, no memory of the thousand things you've already done elsewhere. LZidentity is the opposite of that. One identity that belongs only to you — and remembers you on every chain you've ever touched, and every one that's still on the way.

recovery

If you lose the keys, you don't lose yourself.

Pick the people (or devices) you trust the most — friends, a hardware wallet in a drawer, a second phone. If you ever lose access, two of them together can bring you back. No seed phrase to hide, no twelve words to forget — your identity is built around the people you'd actually call in a panic.

trading

Real markets, without leaving home.

Trade perpetuals on Hyperliquid from inside your identity — a live order book, candles that move with the market, your positions and balance in one place. Every order is signed by your own wallet and settles on-chain. Start on testnet, switch to mainnet when it feels right.

assistant

Someone who knows the whole app.

An assistant tied to your identity that actually understands the app. Ask it to explain a feature, open a tab, read your balances, or set up a trade for you to sign. Bring your own Claude key for the full thing, or lean on the built-in guide. It never signs anything for you.

The app speaks three "languages" so your message can always find a way through. You don't have to choose — it picks the best one for each message. Or override with a long-press.
